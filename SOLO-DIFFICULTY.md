Solo Tapsee difficulty

Open Single Player, choose Tapsee, then Easy, Normal or Hard before Start Single Player. The choice is stored in preferences and captured at gameplay start. Normal is the previous accepted balance. The selector applies to AI Don in this mode; solo Don hunt and human LAN matches keep their existing rules.

Easy gives slower awareness growth, shorter search memory and smaller/slower searches. Hard gives quicker awareness growth, longer memory and wider/more frequent searches. Hearing range varies modestly, retaining native same-floor and occlusion checks. Chase movement speed, capture reach, five-second phone call, rescue countdown and player movement do not vary. Crouching remains more forgiving in every preset.

Validation: 12 pure difficulty/awareness assertions; 138 native AI path/search/perception assertions under all presets; 59 native gameplay regressions. Separately, actual engine-frame agent movement reached 14 route/breaker stops with 44 assertions. Menu visual/touch acceptance, full AI playthroughs, device performance and two-device LAN are still pending.
