# Basement stealth interactions — source 0.3.8/code38

This pass adds three gameplay choices inside the existing basement, in solo and LAN. The mansion is not rebuilt.

- Laundry linen trolley: an opaque cloth-covered trolley in the laundry room's front-right area (9, -3.35, 3.75). Use the existing hide action to enter; a 16 cm front viewing slot and low camera allow peeking. Use the action to leave. Existing seen/grounded-entry and blocked-exit checks apply. Don can search it through the existing hiding-spot system. Its hollow collider shell has a separate filled navigation exclusion. Total hides: 13.
- Hand-cranked radio: a detailed radio on a small game-room console (-4.25, -2.5, -0.85). Wind it to produce eight seconds of positional noise, even without mains power. It can be reused after 24 seconds. AI Don investigates the sound location only if he can hear it on the same storey, with wall attenuation and difficulty hearing applied; it does not override an active chase or reveal the player's actual position. Human players hear the same noise and decide how to react.
- Workshop supply drawer: a small parts drawer on the existing workbench (7.15, -2.25, -5.65). One spare battery per role restores up to 25% / 75 seconds of flashlight capacity. Full batteries do not consume the spare, charge caps at full, and an exhausted torch does not switch on automatically. Visible cells and the drawer front update with collection state.

Both modes use the existing action control rather than adding touch buttons. Radio timing, spare allowances and flashlight charge are host-authoritative in LAN and included in snapshots. Network protocol is version 3, requiring matching builds. Radio crackle is original procedural synthesis; existing material assets remain subject to their original licences.
