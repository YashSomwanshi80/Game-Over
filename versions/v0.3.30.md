## What changed

- **Fixes the blurry picture on phones from v0.3.29.** That version used Android's Fixed DPI scaling, which rendered the game at about 127x58 pixels on a 2780x1264 screen. It is switched off again.
- **New render size, computed from each device's own screen** (no fixed resolution):
  - High 0.85, Medium 0.75 and Low 0.65 of the native size.
  - The short side never drops below 720 px, and screens of 720 px or less are never scaled down.
  - Aspect ratio is always kept. Desktop is untouched.

## Checks

- 48 logic checks pass, including a sweep of 288 screen and scale combinations (never larger than the screen, never below 720 px on the short side, aspect kept).
- On the phone, the graphics buffer was exactly 2363x1074 on High.
