## What changed

- New **GRAPHICS** tab in Settings (main menu and pause menu).
- Frame-rate cap: 30, 45 or 60 FPS.
- Graphics quality: Low, Medium or High. Lower levels use fewer lights and shadows, a shorter shadow distance and no edge smoothing. Low turns shadows off.
- Defaults are unchanged: 60 FPS and High. Changes apply instantly and are remembered.
