## What changed

- **Cooler graphics on phones.** The same GRAPHICS levels now do more work for less heat:
  - High: 3 pixel lights (was 4), 7 active room lights (was 9), 1 shadow-casting room light (was 2), 12 m shadow distance (was 16), no multisampling (was 2x).
  - Shadow maps are low resolution on phones. Lightning and police lights no longer cast shadows on phones.
  - Render scale on phones: 0.85 High, 0.75 Medium, 0.65 Low (Fixed DPI scaling is now enabled).
  - Desktop values are unchanged.
- **Minimap:** the doorway walls (Storage, Laundry, Utility, Safe room, washrooms) and door side panels are now drawn. They were missing before.

## Checks

- Full game compiles with Unity 6000.3.21f1 for Android and PC.
- 38 logic checks pass: 4-second call, 0.9 m / 100 degree capture, office second exit, minimap walls, and the graphics tiers (desktop unchanged, phone lighter, High >= Medium >= Low).

## Known issues

- Heat and battery are not yet measured on a real phone. The new render scale makes the on-screen HUD slightly softer.
