## Audit and priorities

Much architecture and set dressing remains generated geometry. Imported scanned furniture and rigged character resources exist, but neither current character fidelity nor additional small props establishes photoreal quality. Native gameplay, visual consistency and real Android measurements are required before a release claim.

The next five priorities are: native regressions and visual acceptance; coherent PBR/material/lighting calibration; first-person and character animation refinement; fair AI/difficulty and environmental interactions; actual Android builds and measured device optimization.
